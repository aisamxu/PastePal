(http://www.generalarcade.com/). Flycut is a fork of Jumpcut. Flycut source code
is available on GitHub at http://github.com/TermiT/Flycut.
Flycut © General Arcade, 2011 - 2020.

PastePal incorporates the following libraries, used with gratitude:
	• ShortcutRecorder by Jesper et al. (<http://wafflesoftware.net/>)
	• a modified version of UKPrefsPanel by Uli Kusterer (<http://www.zathras.de/>)
	• SGHotKeysLib by Justin Williams (<https://github.com/agilebits/SGHotKeysLib>)

CommonCrypto system frameworks.
